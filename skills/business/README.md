# Business

General business skills that are not tied to one of Aaron's businesses: SEO, marketing and growth, business strategy and consulting operations, GovCon capture, and business tax.

Each subfolder is a registry domain (`seo`, `growth`, `strategy`, `govcon`, `legal-financial`). A skill here lives at `skills/business/<domain>/<skill>/SKILL.md`.

Skills built around E7 or TempleProtocol (their offers, tiers, lanes, or owner context) stay out of this folder and wait for a separate private repository. See `MOVED.md` for the 2026-11-27 move and its compatibility links.
