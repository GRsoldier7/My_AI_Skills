# Content Formats

Choose the requested format. These are editorial defaults, not platform limits.

## Single post

Use one honest opening, essential context, one or two textual observations, fitting application, and optional reflection question. Usually 120–220 words. Include passage reference and translation for quotations. Do not invent personal history to make the opening relatable.

## Carousel

Default six slides: question/message; passage/context; two insights; application; takeaway/reflection. One point per slide, usually 15–35 words. Add a caption carrying context and source details. Avoid an isolated misleading verse as title card. Supply accessible descriptions when requested. Text slides/visual instructions are not created images.

## Short video

Separate spoken copy, on-screen references, and optional visual beats. Start around 120–150 spoken words for an approximately 60-second script; label duration an estimate and adapt to actual delivery. Use question, context, insight, response. Do not stage testimony or force emotional intensity. Check audio/video quotation and attribution requirements.

## Newsletter/devotional

Usually 400–700 words: message, context, explanation, relevant labeled illustration, application, optional prayer, source notes. Do not add unrelated moral advice to fill length. A devotional is interpretation, not additional Scripture.

## Series/bundle

Use a checked study brief or clear passage sequence. Give distinct teaching purposes rather than repeated hooks. Table: sequence/date, passage, message, audience/format, review status. Without a date request use sequence numbers. Do not claim reminders or posts are queued.

For a requested study bundle, create a post, carousel outline, and short script from the same supported message. Keep study and personal response primary, not content volume.

## Review

Does the reader understand the passage's meaning before application? Did compression preserve material qualifications? Is the hook as honest as the body? Are quotations and experiences traceable? Could combined details identify a private person? Does the format aid understanding without manipulation? Is draft/outline status distinct from rendering or publishing?
