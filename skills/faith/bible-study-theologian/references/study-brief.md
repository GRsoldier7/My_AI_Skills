# Study Brief

Use for interpretation handoff to teaching, prayer, or publishing. For a short post or prayer, three to five lines may suffice: passage, contextual message, indispensable qualification, fitting response, and source basis. Use the fuller fields below only when useful for a group, series, or complex study. Do not output an internal brief unless requested. This is a data contract, not a mandatory answer template.

```text
Passage and translation:
Text basis: retrieved / user-supplied / reference-only summary
Audience and requested deliverable:
Main message in context:
Essential setting and passage movement:
Two to four insights: verse -> observation -> interpretation
Cross-references and each contribution:
Alternatives or unresolved claims:
Application: principle -> fitting response
Public-safe material only:
Source receipts: consulted URL/title/location for claims requiring verification
Review state: draft / text-checked / human-reviewed, only when performed
Next question/passage, if known:
```

Downstream skills must support new claims and preserve qualifications. Reuse valid interpretation instead of repeating the entire study. Keep private journal/group data out by default. Text-checked does not mean approved to publish.

With only a passage, do the minimum necessary grounding. With supplied notes, inspect and repair errors first. With missing notes, ask for them instead of inventing their contents.
