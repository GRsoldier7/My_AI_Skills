# Banned phrases

Tags: **CUT** = always cut. **UNLESS** = cut unless literal or technical. Quoted text and code are never edited.

## Hype words
- CUT: groundbreaking, game-changer, game-changing, revolutionary, cutting-edge, breathtaking, stunning, unparalleled, world-class, best-in-class, seamless, holistic, transformative, paradigm shift, must-visit, renowned, unleash, supercharge, invaluable
- CUT: delve, tapestry, testament, beacon, multifaceted, meticulous, pivotal, paramount, vibrant, nestled, in the heart of, ever-evolving, quintessential
- UNLESS: robust (statistics sense), crucial (literal need), key (adjective), comprehensive (scope is stated), innovative, notable, profound, intricate, landscape (physical), gate/gated (technical), streamline (a named process step), leverage (finance or physics)

## Filler adverbs
- CUT: really, just, literally, genuinely, honestly, simply, truly, deeply, fundamentally, inherently, inevitably, interestingly, importantly, crucially, remarkably, surprisingly, quietly
- UNLESS: actually (corrects a stated belief), significantly (a measured statistic), very, quite, highly

## Throat-clearing openers
- CUT: "Here's the thing", "Here's what", "Here's why", "The truth is", "The real question is", "It turns out", "Let me be clear", "To be clear", "I'm going to be honest", "Let's dive in", "Let's break this down", "Without further ado", "It's worth noting", "It's important to note", "At its core", "At the end of the day", "When it comes to", "In today's [anything]", "In a world where", "The reality is", "Look,", "Real talk", "Honestly?", "Make no mistake", "Think about it:", "Can we talk about"
- CUT: "In this article", "In this section, we'll", "As we'll see", "The rest of this", "Let me walk you through"

## Staged contrasts and setups
- CUT: "not X, it's Y", "not just X but Y", "not only X but also Y", "isn't X. It's Y.", "The question isn't X", "stops being X and starts being Y", "doesn't mean X. It means Y."
- CUT: "Plot twist", "Spoiler", "What if I told you", "Here's what nobody tells you", "What most people get wrong", "The part everyone misses"
- CUT: "Let that sink in", "Read that again", "Full stop", "Period.", "That's the real win", "That distinction matters"

## Chatbot residue
- CUT: "Great question", "That's a great", "Certainly!", "Of course!", "Absolutely!", "You're absolutely right", "I hope this helps", "Hope this helps", "As an AI", "Here is an overview of", "Sure, here"
- CUT: "As of my last update", "up to my last training", "based on available information", "specific details are limited", "not widely documented", "maintains a low profile"

## Closers
- CUT: "Let me know if", "Feel free to", "Don't hesitate to", "Happy to help", "Would you like me to", "Want me to", "Shall I", "If you'd like, I can", "If you want, I can", "I can also", "Should I continue", "Here are a few options"
- CUT: "In conclusion", "In summary", "Ultimately,", "Overall,", "The bottom line is", "The key takeaway", "The future looks bright", "Exciting times ahead", "a step in the right direction"

## Inflated significance
- CUT: "stands as a testament", "marks a pivotal moment", "plays a vital role", "plays a key role", "underscores its importance", "solidifies its position", "indelible mark", "enduring legacy", "setting the stage for", "evolving landscape", "continues to thrive"
- CUT trailing -ing add-ons that only claim meaning: highlighting, underscoring, emphasizing, showcasing, reflecting, fostering, cultivating, symbolizing

## Weasel attribution
- CUT: "experts agree", "experts suggest", "studies show", "research suggests", "many believe", "some argue", "industry reports", "widely regarded as", "some critics"
- Fix: name the source and date, or cut the claim. Unsourced and needed: mark [VERIFY].
