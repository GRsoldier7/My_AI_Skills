## Faith and CustomGPT continuity

Preserved: Scripture as final authority, inerrancy in the original manuscripts, the Trinity, Christ’s full deity and humanity, salvation by grace through faith, the priesthood of believers, and ESV preference. The skills use a Protestant/Baptist lens without inventing positions on election, spiritual gifts, or end times.

The inherited teaching approach is observation → interpretation → application, with warm mentoring, two to four substantial insights, warranted connections to Christ, and concrete personal and communal response. Concise answers stay concise; deep research can expand through references. Prayer supports the user’s own engagement with God, Scripture, and church.

The accessible packaged instructions were inspected; this was not a live CustomGPT configuration or conversation-history export. The live CustomGPT and installed plugins were not edited. The newer mentor’s contextual word-study rules and flexible output structure take precedence over the older template’s root-meaning shortcuts, mandatory seven sections, and unsupported persistent-memory assumptions.

## What changed in each skill

**Prayer and Reflection.** Adds a contextual worked prayer, distinguishes an interactive prompt from a complete timed routine, and strengthens grace-centered confession and privacy. Review caught a status label that could imply an answer without an outcome; the tightened rule and fresh rerun preserve ongoing status and the user’s interpretation separately.

**Faith and Life Integration.** Keeps decisions and rhythms grounded in contextual Scripture, adds a concrete spaced-memory example with a manageable review queue, and strengthens busy-day/restart behavior and personal/communal application.

**Group Study Leader.** Keeps complete leader and participant materials, exact time budgets and voluntary sharing. Adds matched questions/notes, a practical mentoring mode, conditional interpretation loading, and honest continuity/fallback behavior.

**Bible Study Publisher.** Reuses checked meaning, shortens internal briefs, loads only requested format guidance, preserves qualifications in hooks and strict limits, and retains privacy, rights and truthful draft-status checks.

**Bible Study Theologian.** Preserves the mentor’s strongest habits, makes the shared foundation reusable, scales the handoff brief, and adds a workflow for meaningful textual variants. Study, contextual-promise, lexical, false-source, missing-history, unavailable-tool and variant tasks were exercised.

**Sunday School and Family Teaching.** Preserves complete lessons rather than outlines, adds positive Trinity/incarnation teaching language, clarifies family/class ownership, and keeps simple activities, age adaptations, grace and repair together.

**Social Media Apologetics.** Moves deeper evidence/engagement methods into a conditional reference, removes the forced Christ-connection closing, and preserves contextual support, warmth, honest evidence and the no-em-dash preference.

## Use and improve

Keep the working CustomGPT. Choose one repo profile per workspace: faith-personal for study/prayer/habits; faith-teaching for groups/family; faith-publishing for original posts/replies; faith-learning only when all seven are useful. Avoid loading overlapping plugin and repo definitions of the same skill.

For each skill, review three real uses before promotion: did it preserve the passage, sound like the mentor you value, save preparation time, and produce something you actually used? Keep sensitive examples privately; record generic lessons and failures in the repo. Review specific weak factors instead of adding more features or chasing a perfect number.
